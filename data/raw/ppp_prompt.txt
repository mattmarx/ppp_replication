- Paper title
- Patent title

- B: They are mostly the same but with some differences
- C: They are only somewhat related
- D: They are entirely unrelated

Please analyze the provided paper and patent and provide your assessment.

Return your response as JSON with exactly this structure (no markdown, no code blocks):
{"assessment": "<assessment>"}

Where:
- assessment: your assessment as A, B, C, or D
